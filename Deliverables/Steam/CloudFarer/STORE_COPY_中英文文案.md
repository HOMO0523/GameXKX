## 简短描述（简体中文）

把一段江湖旅程放在桌面上。《霞客行》是一款融合桌面挂机与卡牌战斗的水墨风角色扮演游戏。安排队伍游历，收集装备与材料，调整卡组和伙伴搭配；闲时积累成长，想认真出手时，再进入战斗亲自决定下一张牌。

## Short description (English)

Keep a little ink-painted adventure on your desktop. CloudFarer blends idle progression with card battles: send your party traveling, collect equipment, and refine your builds. Let your companions make progress, then step into battle and choose your next card.

## 详细介绍（简体中文）

### 一段留在桌面上的江湖旅程

安排队伍出发游历，在水墨风的桌面界面中查看战况与成长。可以让队伍自动战斗，也可以进入挑战，亲自思考出牌顺序和目标。

### 搭配自己的队伍与卡组

围绕主角、伙伴与 NPC 调整编队和卡组。不同角色与卡牌提供不同的战斗选择，尝试适合自己的攻防节奏。

### 让装备成为构筑的一部分

收集装备、宝石与材料，整理背包和仓库，使用强化、镶嵌等工具调整配置，再把新的搭配带回战斗中验证。

### 在游历与挑战之间切换

通过历练、任务和角色教学逐步熟悉玩法，积累资源并解锁成长。既可以留出一段轻松挂机的时间，也可以在需要时专注于眼前的一场战斗。

## About This Game (English)

### An ink-painted journey on your desktop

Send your party traveling and follow their progress through a desktop interface inspired by Chinese ink painting. Let battles play out automatically, or enter a challenge and take control of card order and targets.

### Build your party and your deck

Combine your hero, companions, and an NPC in a party, then adjust their card loadouts. Explore different approaches to offense and defense as you find a rhythm that suits you.

### Make equipment part of your strategy

Collect gear, gems, and materials. Organize your backpack and warehouse, use equipment tools to refine your build, and bring your choices back into battle.

### Alternate between travel and challenge

Learn through training, tasks, and character tutorials as you gather resources and develop your party. Leave room for relaxed idle play, or focus on a battle when you want to make the decisions yourself.
